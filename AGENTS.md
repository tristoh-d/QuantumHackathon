# AGENTS

Quantum Hackathon project: TVA power-plant scheduling (unit commitment) with a hybrid MILP / QAOA approach.
See README.md, docs/challenge.md and docs/architecture.md.

- `src/check.py` is the organizers' checker: do not modify it (only its DATA path differs from upstream).
- `reference/upstream/` holds untouched copies of the organizers' files: do not edit.
- The TODOs (TODO 1-3 in src/classical.py and src/qubo.py, two in src/maxcut.py) are the team's to write.
- Run tests with `pytest` from the repo root; experiments with `python -m experiments.<name>`.
